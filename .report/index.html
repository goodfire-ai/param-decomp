Setup

This branch (feature/layerwise-parallel-training) decomposes a 4-block target LM module-by-module: one independent SLURM task per target module, sharing only the frozen target. The Pareto we care about is:

  • L0 (eval/l0/0.0_total) — sparsity / how many components are "on" per datapoint. Lower = sparser.
  • PGD recon (eval/loss/PGDReconLoss) — adversarial mask robustness via 20-step PGD. Lower = more robust.
  • Stoch recon (eval/loss/StochasticHiddenActsReconLoss) — random-mask reconstruction loss.

Subject to keeping Faithfulness (params → components reconstruction) low. We want to push the (L0, recon) frontier down-and-left.

3:1 (comp 3× faster than CI) is consistently best. At every imp_min level it has the lowest PGD recon. But the three viable ratios (10:1, 3:1, 1:3) are close — split LRs are not dramatically reshaping the frontier on block 2.

Key findings

  • More steps = strictly better Pareto. 10k → 100k moves average L0 from ~21 to ~13 and average PGD recon from ~0.68 to ~0.35. Convergence not reached at 100k — v1's 200k runs (in flight) should keep moving.
  • Two modules are stuck at much-higher faithfulness loss: attn.k_proj and attn.q_proj sit at 5e-6 while every other module reaches ~5e-9 (3 orders of magnitude worse). These are also the modules with much higher Stoch recon (~0.4 vs ~0.02). Likely a capacity issue — they are the only modules at C=512.
  • mlp.down_proj is the outlier in the other direction. It is already very sparse (L0 ≈ 11-13) and L0 actually increases slightly with more steps — likely a sparsity/recon balance issue at the high-coeff end of the imp-min knob.
  • PPGD at 10k tracks no-PPGD closely on this slice — the comparison is more informative at longer step counts (in flight).
